About Me

The AI-Integrated Smart Hydroponics System is an advanced agricultural solution designed to improve crop productivity through automation, real-time monitoring, and data-driven decision-making. By combining Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT) technology, and hydroponic cultivation techniques, the system enables efficient plant growth with minimal resource consumption. Our Solution Our system uses smart sensors, AI algorithms, and automated control mechanisms to continuously monitor and manage critical growing conditions such as nutrient levels, pH, temperature, humidity, water quality, and lighting. The platform analyzes real-time data and automatically adjusts system parameters to maintain optimal growing conditions for crops. .
